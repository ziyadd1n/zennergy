THE STORY BEHIND ZEN ENERGY

Industry roots.
Open horizons.

A platform born from an oil and gas background, and a curiosity about everything that comes next.

ZEN energy puts oil and gas expertise at the heart of a broader energy conversation. We bring upstream activity, refining, natural gas, LNG, renewables and policy into one shared news and insights feed.

For professionals, students and anyone who wants to understand energy better, this is a place to keep learning and see the connections.
